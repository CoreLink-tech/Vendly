How it works

Up and running
in three steps

No tech experience needed. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Vendly.

🏪
1
Create your store

Sign up with your email, choose your store name, and you have a live link — vendly.com/yourname. Takes less than 2 minutes. No setup, no tech knowledge required.

📦
2
Add your products

Upload a photo, type the name and price. Done. Add as many products as you sell, and update them anytime from your dashboard — right from your phone.

💬
3
Share and receive orders

Send your store link on WhatsApp status, Instagram bio, anywhere. Customers pick what they want and their full order lands directly in your WhatsApp inbox.
